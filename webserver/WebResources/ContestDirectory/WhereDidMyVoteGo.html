Where did my vote go? PROTOTYPE

Instructions for this page

This page enables one to see who your (or any other) vote ended up helping elect, tracking through the preferences. You enter a vote, and the page will show how it is affected by the exclusions and surplus distributions that actually happened.

If you enter a vote which actually was one of the votes counted in the election, then the following should be accurate. If you enter a vote that was not one of the votes counted, this page will attempt to show what would have happened with such a vote, assuming that the vote didn't change the course of the election (usually but not always a decent approximation).

The formality requirements of a minimum number of preferences will not be enforced.

Usage:

  • You can enter numbers by typing in the boxes adjacent to a name. Duplicated numbers will get a red background.
  • As a shortcut, clicking on the name (not the box) of a candidate will assign the candidate the best preference not already assigned.
  • Holding down the ALT key while clicking on a name of a candidate will assign the worst preference not already assigned.
  • If the box already contains a number when you click on a name, the current number in the box will be cleared.
  • If you wish to save a partially completed ballot to resume later, record the numbers in the summary section. Then next time you visit this page, copy the saved numbers into the summary field. This uses the same format as the Prepare Vote page.
  • Currently only below the line votes are supported.
